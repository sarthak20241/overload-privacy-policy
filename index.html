Privacy Policy for Overload

Last updated: December 2025

Introduction

Overload ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Overload mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

Personal Information

We collect the following information when you use our Service:

  • Account Information: Username, email address (optional)
  • Workout Data: Exercise names, weights, reps, sets, timers, notes
  • Usage Data: App usage statistics, crash reports

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our Service
  • To allow you to track your workout progress
  • To provide AI-powered workout generation and coaching (via Google Gemini AI)
  • To improve our Service

Data Storage

  • All workout data is stored securely in MongoDB Atlas
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • MongoDB Atlas: Data storage
  • Google Gemini AI: Workout generation and coaching insights
  • AWS Lambda: Backend hosting (serverless)

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • JWT tokens for authentication
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • No payment information is collected or stored

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your workout data

Data Retention

  • Workout data is retained as long as your account is active
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time

Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. This page explains how to delete your Overload account and all associated data.

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. This will delete:

  • Your account and profile information
  • All workout routines
  • All workout history and sessions
  • All progress data and analytics

How to Delete Your Account

1 Open the Overload App

Launch the Overload app on your iOS or Android device and make sure you're logged in.

2 Go to Settings

Navigate to the "Workouts" tab and tap the settings icon (⚙️) in the top right corner.

3 Delete Your Account

Tap "Delete Account" and confirm your decision. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted.

💡 Tip: If you have the app installed, you can delete your account directly from within the app using the steps above.

Alternative: Request Deletion via Email

If you don't have access to the app or prefer to request deletion via email, you can contact us at:

Email: allhopesalive@gmail.com

Please include your username in the email so we can locate and delete your account.

We will process your deletion request within 7 business days.

What Happens After Deletion?

  • Your account will be immediately and permanently deleted
  • All your workout data will be removed from our servers
  • You will no longer be able to log in or recover any data
  • You can create a new account at any time if you change your mind

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or need assistance, please contact us at allhopesalive@gmail.com.
